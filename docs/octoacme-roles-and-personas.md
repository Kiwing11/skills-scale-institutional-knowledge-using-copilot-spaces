# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA / Testing Lead owns acceptance testing strategy, quality standards, and the test automation roadmap. This role acts as a quality gatekeeper by collaborating with Developers and Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test automation strategy and coverage targets
- Review acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers for testability
- Manage QA capacity and testing timelines
- Conduct manual QA and oversee regression testing
- Escalate quality blockers and release risks
- Report quality metrics and trends

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Enable fast feedback cycles for development teams
- Reduce testing cycle time while maintaining confidence

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with Developers on testability and defect triage
- Acceptance criteria reviews with Product Managers
- Quality risk escalations to Project Managers and Product Leads

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead / Architecture Owner provides technical direction, design decisions, and cross-component integration oversight. This role is the go-to authority for technical risks and architectural trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture decisions
- Review designs and architectural trade-offs
- Manage cross-component dependencies and integration concerns
- Identify and escalate technical risks early
- Mentor Developers on technical practices and standards
- Participate in code reviews and recommend technology selections

### Goals
- Ensure scalable and maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid brittle designs
- Minimize integration issues across teams and components

### Typical Communication
- Design and implementation collaboration with Developers
- Technical feasibility and risk discussions with Project Managers and Product Leads
- Architectural risk escalations when major trade-offs are required

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
The Product Lead owns strategic alignment across multiple projects and teams. This role manages competing priorities, cross-team dependencies, and broader product strategy decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure strategic alignment across initiatives
- Manage competing priorities and resource conflicts
- Identify and escalate cross-team dependencies
- Approve major scope and timeline changes
- Make trade-off decisions between competing initiatives
- Represent business and stakeholder interests
- Review and approve project charters

### Goals
- Maximize strategic value and business impact
- Reduce rework and misalignment across teams
- Enable faster go/no-go and prioritization decisions

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and scope reviews with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Dependency and priority alignment with other Product Leads
- Strategic escalations to Stakeholders / Sponsors

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder / Sponsor is the business owner and decision authority who provides budget, strategic alignment, and business context. This role represents executive leadership interests in projects.

### Responsibilities
- Approve business case and project budget
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Make go/no-go decisions at project gates
- Communicate project status and outcomes to executive leadership
- Approve major releases and external announcements
- Escalate business-critical issues and remove organizational blockers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Reduce risk through timely decisions at key gates
- Align portfolio outcomes with strategic priorities

### Typical Communication
- Approval and alignment discussions during Project Charter review
- Escalation intake from Product Leads on strategic decisions
- Executive updates on outcomes, risks, and major milestones

---

## Security / Compliance Owner

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Owner defines security requirements, reviews designs for vulnerabilities, and owns incident response protocols. This role is the authority for security and regulatory compliance decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Review system designs for security implications
- Manage security scanning and vulnerability remediation workflows
- Own incident response protocols and triage expectations
- Ensure regulatory compliance requirements are met
- Serve as on-call contact for security incidents
- Provide security training and guidance to teams

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements
- Enable fast, coordinated incident response

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews with Technical Leads and Developers
- Incident notifications, triage updates, and remediation coordination
- Security risk escalations to Product Leads and Stakeholders / Sponsors
